# Block 0 - Why Architecture Matters (0:00-4:04)

Source: study guide, based on a video that builds a Google Drive clone, evolving the architecture step by step (see docs/04-limitations.md for the missing citation of the original video).

## Core idea

A poor architecture makes adding new capabilities progressively slower and more expensive. Architecture is not a technical luxury: it exists to sustain the business.

## Key concepts

- Architecture means understanding what you are building, not only how.
- Every decision has trade-offs; there is no single universally correct architecture.
- The optimal architecture depends on the stage of the company.
- The opposite risk is over-engineering.

## Study question (from the source)

Why can an architecture that works for a startup with 10 users be the wrong one for the same product at 10,000 users?

## Note

This block is framed in the guide as the motivation for everything that follows: each later block introduces one new architectural piece, in response to a specific problem caused by growth.
